The mark

Banksia is the Australian wildflower genus that feeds the hive: nectar-rich, fire-resilient, and named for Joseph Banks. The mark is a banksia spike built from honeycomb cells and grounded by its woody cone.

The spike is Hive red, so the app reads as part of the Hive family at a glance without reusing the Hive logo. It still reads as a flower, a hive and a single cell at once — a plant that survives fire and comes back.

Hive red is the brand colour: use it for the spike, primary actions and active states.

Light red is the cell highlight; deep maroon grounds the cone and works for quiet detail.

Keep the chain's own Hive badge separate from the app mark; this is a Hive-built product, not the chain itself.

Prefer the dark lockup on Hive Dark; the light lockup on paper and warm surfaces.

Clear space: at least one cell radius on every side. Minimum mark size: 24px; favicon: 16px.

Do not rotate, stretch or recolour the spike. Do not place the lockup on a busy photo without a scrim.
